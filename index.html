AI infrastructure & storage

Vipul Patil

I work on the layer underneath the models — where data is stored, indexed, and pulled back fast enough to matter.

I spent two years at Veritas (now Cohesity) keeping enterprise NetBackup running in production: backups, replication, failover, disaster recovery, for customers whose data was genuinely on the line. I'm now finishing an M.S. in Computer Science at UIC, and this past summer I was at Argonne National Laboratory, benchmarking vector databases for multimodal search on the Aurora and Polaris supercomputers.

Storage found me through databases and never let go. What interests me now is where it's heading: vector databases, AI-native storage, and retrieval that holds up at scale.
